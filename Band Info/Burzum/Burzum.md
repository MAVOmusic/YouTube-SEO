# Burzum - artist research

## Project and genres

Burzum is a Norwegian solo project associated with Varg Vikernes. Its catalogue includes black metal and ambient music. Artist-wide working playlist genres: Ambient, Dungeon synth, Black metal, Atmospheric black metal, Dark ambient. These labels are subjective and do not imply every track fits every genre.

Sources: https://rateyourmusic.com/artist/burzum ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burzum

## Main-release discography reference

Years below are release years, not recording dates; this is not every issue, compilation, demo or reissue. Cross-check a specific edition before using its credits or release date. Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burzum ; https://www.allmusic.com/artist/mn0000645956 ; https://musicbrainz.org/artist/49cd96a6-42c3-44f6-ba2a-cd9301046b96 .

- Burzum (1992); Det som engang var (1993); Hvis lyset tar oss (1994); Filosofem (1996).
- Dauði Baldrs (1997); Hliðskjálf (1999); Belus (2010); Fallen (2011).
- Umskiptar (2012); Sôl austan, Mâni vestan (2013); The Ways of Yore (2014); Thulêan Mysteries (2020); The Land of Thulê (2024, Wikipedia main-release listing; verify classification/edition independently before use).
- Aske (1993) is an EP; From the Depths of Darkness (2011) includes re-recordings and is not simply another album of new songs. Check edition and classification if citing.

The first four albums were recorded between 1992 and 1993 but released over several years; recording and release dates are distinct. The project name comes from a fictional language in Tolkien's work. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burzum .
