## Band facts and discography leads

- The group was called Sweet Children before adopting Green Day in 1989. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Day
- Mike Dirnt plays bass; Tre Cool joined on drums in 1990. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Day
- American Idiot is a punk rock opera following the fictional Jesus of Suburbia. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Day
