### LF 0706 / Short 2453 - Transatlanticism

- Title track of the band's fourth album Transatlanticism (7 October 2003, Barsuk), a concept album about long-distance romance that uses the Atlantic Ocean as a metaphor for geographic and emotional separation. Produced by Chris Walla; the band's first album with drummer Jason McGerr; certified Platinum in the US.
