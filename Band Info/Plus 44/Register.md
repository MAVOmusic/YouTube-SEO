## Names, history and evidence

- +44 (Plus 44) included vocalist/bassist Mark Hoppus, drummer Travis Barker and guitarists Craig Fairbaugh and Shane Gallagher. Carol Heller contributed to the early band and to Make You Smile. Source: https://www.allmusic.com/artist/-44-mn0000484990 ; https://www.discogs.com/release/2052863-44-When-Your-Heart-Stops-Beating
- Hoppus and Barker are also members of blink-182; this does not mean blink-182 are the credited artist for these songs. Source: https://www.allmusic.com/artist/-44-mn0000484990
