## Song fact and usage index

### LF 0747 / Short 2494 - For Whom The Bell Tolls

- From Ride the Lightning (1984), inspired by Ernest Hemingway's 1940 novel and the Spanish Civil War scene in its Chapter 27. Cliff Burton plays the introduction with heavy distortion and wah; he wrote it before joining Metallica and first played it in a 1979 battle of the bands jam, recording three bass tracks (lead, clean and harmonics). The opening bell is Lars Ulrich striking an anvil combined with a sound-effects bell.
- Rolling Stone ranked it number 39 on its 100 Greatest Heavy Metal Songs of All Time in 2023.
