# Band Info

Research and usage registers by artist. Before a new song, consult its register for the consistent artist-level genre list and prior fact usage; then research the song, record new sourced findings and CO usage here, and check claims against original evidence before using them. Artist-level genres do not prove a recording belongs to every genre. Name-variant observations do not establish search frequency. New artists require a sourced register. Registers are not exhaustive.

Before writing tags, check artist registers for relevant, source-supported artists, members, prominent aliases and collaborators. Verify the era and relationship; a band member is not automatically a performer on a particular recording. Do not claim search frequency or ranking benefit without evidence. Never print CO-0026 explicit EP or track titles.
