## Band facts and discography leads

- Mikey Way is the bassist; Gerard Way is the vocalist. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Chemical_Romance
- The Black Parade is a 2006 concept album; Welcome to the Black Parade was its lead single. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Chemical_Romance
- The band announced its breakup in 2013 and its reunion in 2019. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Chemical_Romance
