- Fifth Misfits single, released on Halloween itself, 31 October 1981, on Danzig's Plan 9 Records in a pressing of 5,000 black vinyl copies; first Misfits release with the Famous Monsters-inspired logo and with Doyle on guitar; B-side Halloween II has lyrics in Latin.
