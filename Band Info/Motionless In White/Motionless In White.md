# Motionless In White - artist reference

American band from Scranton, Pennsylvania. Artist-wide working playlist genres: metalcore, gothic metal, industrial metal, nu metal and hard rock. These are subjective catalogue-wide descriptors, not a claim that each song fits all five. See [Register.md](Register.md) for song-specific sources, variant names and prior copy.

## Studio album overview

Creatures (2010), Infamous (2012), Reincarnate (2014), Graveyard Shift (2017), Disguise (2019), Scoring the End of the World (2022), and Decades (2026). [Discography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motionless_in_White_discography); [artist Bandcamp listing for Decades](https://officialmotionlessinwhite.bandcamp.com/album/decades). Check editions and release dates separately for specific claims.

## Discussion and research

The band name was inspired by an Eighteen Visions song, according to [Songkick](https://www.songkick.com/artists/2053409-motionless-in-white).
