# Motionless In White - Another Life

Another Life appears on the 2019 album Disguise. Chris Motionless said the band listened to fan feedback when choosing it as a single; Max Moore directed the official video. [Blabbermouth](https://blabbermouth.net/news/motionless-in-white-releases-music-video-for-another-life) reports both facts, independently corroborated for album and director by [Outburn](https://outburn.com/news/motionless-in-white-release-another-life-video/). The official video is distinct from MAVO's performances.
