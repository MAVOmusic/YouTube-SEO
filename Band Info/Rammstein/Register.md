### CO-0015 - 0015 -  Rammstein - Du Hast (Long Form)

- Du Hast is the second single from Sehnsucht, 1997, and classified industrial metal / Neue Deutsche Haerte.
  - Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Du_hast
- Lyrics use wording resembling German wedding vows.
  - Source: https://rammstein.fandom.com/wiki/Du_hast_(song)

### CO-0040 - 0040 - Rammstein - Amerika (Long Form)

- A 2004 edition of Rammstein's Amerika single includes the song and an English version. The track is on Reise, Reise as well.
  - Source: https://rateyourmusic.com/release/single/rammstein/amerika/ ; https://rammstein.fandom.com/wiki/Amerika_(song) ; https://www.discogs.com/release/899138-Rammstein-Amerika

### CO-0063 - 0063 - Rammstein - Hilf Mir (Long Form)

- A review of Rammstein's 2005 album Rosenrot connects the lyrics of Hilf Mir to the Pauline story in Heinrich Hoffmann's Struwwelpeter. The title means "Help me" in English.
  - Source: https://vampster.com/cd-reviews/rammstein-rosenrot/ ; https://genius.com/Rammstein-hilf-mir-lyrics
