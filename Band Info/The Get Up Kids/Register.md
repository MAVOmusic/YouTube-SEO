## Song fact and usage index

### LF 0701 / Short 2448 - Holiday

- Opening track of Something to Write Home About (28 September 1999, Vagrant), the second-wave emo landmark that sold over 100,000 copies, reached number 31 on Billboard Heatseekers, and made Vagrant a central emo label; its only single was Action & Action.
