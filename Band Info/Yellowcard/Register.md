## Fighting - new pair, 2026-11-10

- Fighting is track 2 on Paper Walls, the sixth Yellowcard album, released 17 July 2007 on Capitol and the band's last for that label. The album page gives the track as 3:00: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paper_Walls
- Paper Walls was recorded January 2 to March 22 2007 at Ocean Studios in Burbank, California, produced by Neal Avron and mixed by Tom Lord-Alge at South Beach Studios in Miami. The same page calls it the first Yellowcard album with Ryan Mendez and the last with Peter Mosely.
- The only single from the album was Light Up the Sky, released 5 June 2007. It reached 32 on Billboard's Adult Top 40.
- The album debuted at 13 on the Billboard 200 with about 40,000 first week copies.
- The band posted ten behind the scenes videos on Myspace while making the record, and Ryan Key described the album as a story about hope and finding yourself again.
- No other copy of Fighting exists in either catalogue or in Master_Shorts_Repository, so this is a first version in both formats.
