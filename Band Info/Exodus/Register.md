## Song fact and usage index

### LF 0734 / Short 2481 - The Toxic Waltz

- The Toxic Waltz is on Fabulous Disaster, the band's third album, released 30 January 1989. Never a single, it became one of the band's best known songs and a near-permanent setlist fixture.
- Gary Holt asked Steve Souza to write about what fans do at their gigs; Souza wrote the lyrics in about 25 minutes as a parody of 1950s and 1960s dance-craze songs after seeing a television compilation advert. The song describes the action in a violent mosh pit.
- Personnel on the recording: Steve "Zetro" Souza, Gary Holt, Rick Hunolt, Rob McKillop (bass) and Tom Hunting. Produced by Marc Senesac, Gary Holt and Rick Hunolt.
