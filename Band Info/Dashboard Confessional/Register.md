## Song fact and usage index

### LF 0700 / Short 2447 - Hands Down

- First recorded acoustically for the So Impossible EP in 2001, telling the story of a date Chris Carrabba had in his late teens; re-recorded with the full band as the lead single of A Mark, a Mission, a Brand, a Scar (2003). Carrabba introduces it live as the best day and date he ever had.
- Peaked at number 8 on US Alternative Airplay and number 60 on the UK Singles Chart.
