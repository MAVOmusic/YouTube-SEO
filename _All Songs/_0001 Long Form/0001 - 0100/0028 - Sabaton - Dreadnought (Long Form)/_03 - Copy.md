## DESCRIPTION (1130 characters)

```text
Sabaton wrote this one for a class of ship, not a single ship. When HMS Dreadnought launched in 1906, every other battleship afloat went obsolete in an afternoon, and the name turned into a whole category that every navy suddenly had to build.

The song keeps its point for the bridge. Jutland, 1916, the one time the dreadnought fleets actually found each other, and the ship that started the entire race was in dock for a refit and missed her own war. Her only real kill had been a submarine she rammed the year before, which is the least battleship thing a battleship ever did.

On bass the discipline is the whole job. The line is a march, even and steady, no showing off, and it has to survive the guitars doubling it without drifting. Rush it and the ship lists.
```
