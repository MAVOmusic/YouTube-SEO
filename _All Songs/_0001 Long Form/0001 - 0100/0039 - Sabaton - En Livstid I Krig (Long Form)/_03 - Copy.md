## DESCRIPTION (1517 characters)

```text
Five minutes and thirty five seconds, and for most of it the bass is doing something very simple on purpose. Carolus Rex is a concept record about a king who lost, and En Livstid I Krig is the soldier's view of what that cost. The part keeps a march under everything, never fills, never shows off, and the second the song lifts into the last chorus the restraint is what makes it land.

The band cut the entire album twice, once in English and once in Swedish. A Lifetime of War is the English title and it is a good translation that gets the sense right and the sound wrong. Swedish consonants are harder, the phrasing sits differently against the riff, and Joakim Broden sounds like he is reporting from inside the war rather than narrating it from a desk. If you only know the English version, this is the one to hear.

History lesson attached: the war is the Thirty Years' War, the king is Gustavus Adolphus, and the Swedish army at that point was mostly not Swedish. The song is about the ones who never came home from a war that had nothing to do with the men fighting it.
```
