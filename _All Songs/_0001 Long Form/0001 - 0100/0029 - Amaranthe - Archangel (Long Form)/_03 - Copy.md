## DESCRIPTION (1240 characters)

```text
Listen to the first eight bars and try to locate the bass before you look at the hands. It sits under everything and in front of nothing, which is the whole job in a song with three vocalists, a guitar and a synth all fighting for the same midrange.

Amaranthe released this as a single on 18 September 2020, two weeks before Manifest, and it sits late on the record. Elize Ryd and Olof Morck wrote it, Jacob Hansen produced, Grupa13 shot the video. None of that explains the arrangement. There are only so many frequencies in a mix and this band spends them on voices, so the low end has to behave.

That is what makes it worth learning. No bass moment exists to practise for. The exercise is staying audible without being noticed, keeping the eighths tied to the kick while three melodies happen above your head. Rush a bar and the floor moves.

🟢 Three vocals and a bass floor, live on Kick most nights: https://kick.com/mavomusic
🟣 Twitch rebroadcasts into a second window for tab hoarders: https://www.twitch.tv/mavomusic
💬 Record mix or live room, which do you trust? Say it in Discord, a boost keeps the doors open: https://discord.gg/ESGsGkN4j2

Manifest followed in October 2020 and the album cut runs ten seconds longer than this.
```
