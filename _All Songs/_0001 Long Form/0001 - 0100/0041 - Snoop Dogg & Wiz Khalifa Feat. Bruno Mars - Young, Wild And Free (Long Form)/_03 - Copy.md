## DESCRIPTION (1496 characters)

```text
The story behind this one is that nobody was trying. The Smeezingtons - Bruno Mars, Philip Lawrence and Ari Levine - had a finished backing track sitting around with no home, and it took an A and R hearing it and insisting it was a single to get it in front of Snoop Dogg, who then took it to Wiz Khalifa, who reportedly did not like it much at first and recorded it anyway.

That is the whole song, and you can hear it. Nothing on it is working hard. Three verses, a chorus that arrives like an open window, and a low end that stays almost apologetic underneath the vocal. The temptation with a track this relaxed is to add movement that is not there in the original, and every time you do, it stops sounding like a summer record and starts sounding like a cover band.

It came out on 11 October 2011 as the lead single from the soundtrack to Mac and Devin Go to High School, on Rostrum with Atlantic, and it went top ten in about a dozen countries. It has also been used at every graduation party since, which is roughly what it was built for.
```
