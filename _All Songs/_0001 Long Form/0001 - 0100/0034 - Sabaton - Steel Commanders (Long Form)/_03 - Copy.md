## DESCRIPTION (1406 characters)

```text
Wargaming paid for this one. Sabaton wrote it for World of Tanks in 2021, filmed a video with a Strv K premium tank, and got their own singer recorded as an in-game voice. It is the least mysterious song in their catalogue and the most honest about what it is: a battle anthem with an invoice attached.

What it does not have is a slow section. There is no bridge that drops out, no key change, no ballad moment before the last chorus. Sabaton usually give you one and this track refuses, which is why three minutes forty four feels shorter than the runtime suggests. The cello on the single is Tina Guo, not a synth pad, and it is doing the work a string section would normally be paid for.

For a bass player the whole job is the eighth-note engine underneath the double kick. Nothing to improvise, no fills that survive a second listen, just a constant rate of fire that has to stay identical at the start and the end. Losing tempo once is audible on this song more than almost any other in the genre.
```
