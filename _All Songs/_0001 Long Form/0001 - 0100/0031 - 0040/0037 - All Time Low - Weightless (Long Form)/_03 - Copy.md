## DESCRIPTION (1520 characters)

```text
"Maybe it's not my weekend, but it's gonna be my year." It is the most quoted line the band ever wrote and it is also the most misread. Weightless is not a song about a good time coming. It is a song about being stuck and refusing to name it.

Jack Barakat has described it as not wanting to grow up and wanting to stay a kid. Alex Gaskarth framed it as being trapped in negative space after a year of exams. Both of those are the same feeling from two directions, and the chorus is where they collide: the line sounds like optimism because the melody sells it that way, and the lyric is a stall.

It was the lead single from Nothing Personal, April 2009, Hopeless Records, produced by Matt Squire, and it got the band onto Rock Band and Guitar Hero 5 in the same year. Three minutes eighteen on the record.

The bass part is the reason this one is worth playing rather than just hearing. It is a moving line under a chord pattern that barely changes, and the whole trick is that it pushes forward while the song describes standing still. Drop a beat and the illusion fails instantly - the part is doing the emotional work the lyric is refusing to do.
```
