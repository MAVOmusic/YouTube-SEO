## DESCRIPTION (1333 characters)

```text
The chorus is a warning and the tempo is a party, and the song never picks a side between them. That tension is the whole record. Red Flag came out in September 2006 as the second single from Billy Talent II, produced by Gavin Brown, and it is three minutes sixteen of a band that could not stop moving.

It nearly did not make the album. The band were unsure it fitted and discussed pushing it to a B-side. It went in, it charted, and it has been the one they open with ever since - which is the standard fate of the song everyone in the room doubts.

Jon Gallant's bass is the part nobody talks about and the part that makes it work. It is a running eighth-note line with almost no variation, driving under Ian D'Sa's guitar rather than answering it, and it holds the same speed through every section including the ones that feel like a gear change. That is harder than a flashy line: there is nowhere to breathe and no bar you can take off.
```
