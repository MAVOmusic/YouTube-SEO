## DESCRIPTION (1396 characters)

```text
Ian D'Sa is the guitar player. He also produced this, and that combination is why it sounds the way it does: a punk band recorded by somebody who has to play the riff himself, so nothing is allowed to get in the way of the riff. The bass sits right under it, locked to the kick, doing a chug rather than a line, and the whole four minutes is built to survive being played loud in a room with a bad PA.

It was the first single from Dead Silence, out on 25 May 2012, and it went to number three on the Canadian rock chart. The video is festival footage rather than a concept, which suits a song whose best idea is a tempo change you can feel coming a bar before it arrives.

This is also the one Billy Talent song where the bass has to be deliberately unambitious. Jon Gallant's job here is to be the floor: no fills, no bounce, nothing that pulls attention off the vocal. Playing it well means resisting every instinct a bass player has, and that is a harder discipline than a busy part.
```
