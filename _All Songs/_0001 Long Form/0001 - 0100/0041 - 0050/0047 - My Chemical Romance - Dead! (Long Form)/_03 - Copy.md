## DESCRIPTION (1521 characters)

```text
There is a doctor in this song and he only gets one job. Late in the bridge, over a riff that has been grinning for three minutes, the line lands: you have got two weeks to live. Everything before it is the patient being sarcastic about his own funeral and what follows is a horn section throwing a party.

The joke is the arrangement. Dead! is the most upbeat thing on The Black Parade, which is an album about a man dying of cancer, and it plays the diagnosis as a punchline with a brass band. Gerard Way wrote the album around that contradiction - joy as a refusal to be sad in public - and here the trick is easiest to hear. If you strip the tempo back it becomes unbearable. Play it at the recorded speed and it sounds like relief.

For the bass that means one instruction: do not slow down. The part is a walking figure for most of the song that has to keep its bounce even while the lyric is describing the end of a life. The discipline is entirely in the feel. There is nothing difficult to play here and a wrong mood has nowhere to hide.

Twenty three October 2006, Reprise, produced by Rob Cavallo. Three minutes fifteen, exactly the album length.
```
