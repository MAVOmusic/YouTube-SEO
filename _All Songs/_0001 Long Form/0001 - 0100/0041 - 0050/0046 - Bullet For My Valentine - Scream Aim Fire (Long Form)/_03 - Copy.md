## DESCRIPTION (1326 characters)

```text
Scream Aim Fire is the title track of Bullet For My Valentine's 2008 album. Matt Tuck described its lyrics as imagining life on a battlefield. The album sessions were interrupted by his vocal problems and tonsil surgery in 2007.

Musically it is a thrash song wearing a metalcore jacket. The verses are a chug pattern at full sprint, the chorus opens up because it has to, and the solo is the one place the album allows itself a breath. The bass part is not a melody and is not trying to be: it doubles the rhythm, sits under the guitars on the low string and holds the tempo together while everything above it panics. That is a job, and doing it properly is the reason the take feels tight rather than merely fast.

Colin Richardson produced and mixed it, as he had done with The Poison. It came out in January 2008 as the first single and title track, and it turned up on the Guitar Hero World Tour setlist the same year.
```

## GENRE FINDINGS (internal - not for playlists)

  Verified: first single and title track, released 21 January 2008 (track released
  digitally 18 December 2007), produced and mixed by Colin Richardson with Matt
  Hyde, tracked at Sonic Ranch in El Paso. Written by the band. Metalcore is the
  working label and the band's own. Thrash metal is genuinely audible here in a way
  it was not on The Poison - the verse pattern and the tempo are thrash, which is
  why it is listed rather than buried as an influence. Melodic metalcore is the
  accurate compound. Dropped: "screamo" (the vocal is harsh, the record is not
  screamo) and "post-hardcore" (wrong lineage for this band).
