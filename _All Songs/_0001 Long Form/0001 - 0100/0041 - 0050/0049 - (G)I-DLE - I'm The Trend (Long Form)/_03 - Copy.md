Third digital single, and the only one of them that was premiered at a concert before it existed as a record. I'm The Trend was played for the first time at the group's online show in July 2020 as the encore, and two days later it was on streaming. It is not a title track, it is not a comeback, it is a fan letter with a beat, and it is the only thing in the catalogue written specifically to be sung back at them.

The credits are the interesting part. This is the one (G)I-DLE single where the writing is not led by Soyeon - Minnie and Yuqi built it with her and with FCM Houdini, and the result is softer, more conversational and much less choreographed than the singles around it. It still ended up on the physical release of DUMDi DUMDi as a CD-only track, which is the most K-pop sentence of 2020.

For the bass the instruction is simple: hold the pocket and do not dress it up. The mix is bright, the synths own the top, and the low end is the only thing keeping the groove from floating off. Nothing here is fast. Everything here is placement.
