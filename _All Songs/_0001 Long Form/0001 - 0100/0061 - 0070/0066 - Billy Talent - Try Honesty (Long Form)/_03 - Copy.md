## DESCRIPTION (1311 characters)

```text
They had already made a record by this point. Watoosh! came out in 1999 under the name Pezz, and by the second album the name was gone, the label was Atlantic, and the first single was a song about being lied to by somebody you trusted.

Try Honesty is the opening statement of that record and it is unusually bitter for a lead single. Ben Kowalewicz spits the verses, Ian D'Sa puts a riff under them that keeps changing shape, and Gavin Brown produces it dry enough that the whole thing sounds like four people in a room arguing.

The bass line is busier than the genre usually allows, which is where Billy Talent gets its character. It walks through the verses, locks with the kick on the chorus, and never quite settles into a groove you could call comfortable. Learn it slowly. Speed is the last thing this part needs and the first thing it tempts you into.

Two thousand and three, Atlantic, four minutes thirteen on the record.
```
