## DESCRIPTION (1257 characters)

```text
Paulinchen is a girl in a German picture book from 1845 who is told not to play with matches, plays with them anyway, and burns to death while the cats watch. Heinrich Hoffmann wrote it to frighten children, and it worked for generations. Rammstein read it as an adult and got this.

The song is five minutes twenty one and moves at roughly the speed of a funeral, and both of those are deliberate. Till Lindemann spends the first half describing the fire with something close to affection, and the last ninety seconds watching it take hold. It is the most uncomfortable thing they have recorded and it knows it.

There is barely a note to play here and everything to place. Long notes, a slow climb, and a chorus that needs weight rather than movement. Get ahead of this one and the whole thing collapses, because the arrangement is built on the sense that nothing is rushing to help her.
```
