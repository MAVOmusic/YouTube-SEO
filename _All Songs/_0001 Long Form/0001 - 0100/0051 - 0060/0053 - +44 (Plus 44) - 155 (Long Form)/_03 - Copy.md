## DESCRIPTION (1548 characters)

```text
It is not a lyric and it is not a reference you are missing. 155 is the tempo. The song sits at 155 beats per minute in F minor, and when the band stripped it back for an acoustic demo they slowed it down and renamed it 145, because the number was never the title, it was the speedometer. Every reworking changed the name to match.

That is a very Mark Hoppus way of naming a song, and it tells you what to expect from the record. The album arrived in November 2006, a year after blink-182 went on indefinite hold, and it was not the pop punk album anyone expected. Jerry Finn produced it with Hoppus and Travis Barker, and the two of them wrote every track. The guitars came from Shane Gallagher and Craig Fairbaugh. What came out was darker, slower in places, and much more interested in texture than in jokes.

On bass, resist playing it like a blink song. At 155 the temptation is to push, and the part works because it holds back and lets the guitars carry the top of the arrangement. The chorus is where the whole thing lifts, and it lifts because nothing under it is fighting.

Thirteen November 2006, Interscope, three minutes twenty nine.
```
