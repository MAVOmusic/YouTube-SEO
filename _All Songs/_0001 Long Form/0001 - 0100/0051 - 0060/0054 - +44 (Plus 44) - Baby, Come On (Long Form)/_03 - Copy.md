## DESCRIPTION (1489 characters)

```text
The record opens with Lycanthrope, which is as close to hardcore as this band ever got, and then track two does a complete about face. Baby, Come On is two minutes forty six of major-key pop with a keyboard hook, and it went out as a single precisely because it is the friendliest thing on a record full of grief.

That contrast is the whole album in miniature. When Your Heart Stops Beating was written in the aftermath of blink-182 going on hold, and it keeps interrupting its own sadness with tunes that would sit happily on a summer radio show. Hoppus and Barker wrote all of it together, Jerry Finn produced, and Gallagher and Fairbaugh filled out the guitars. The band was never a side project in the throwaway sense. It was two people working out what to do next, in public.

For the bass, play this one clean. No distortion, no fill at the end of the bar, just the root and the bounce. The keyboard is doing the melody, the guitar is doing the jangle, and the low end exists to make the whole thing move. Play one note too many and it stops being a pop song.

Thirteen November 2006, Interscope, two minutes forty six.
```
