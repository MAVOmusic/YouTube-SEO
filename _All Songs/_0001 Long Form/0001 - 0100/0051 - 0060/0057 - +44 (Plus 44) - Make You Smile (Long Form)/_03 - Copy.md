## DESCRIPTION (1611 characters)

```text
One review of the album said the band should have split when Carol Heller left, because she was the only thing making them different. It is a harsh line and it lands, because Make You Smile is the one track where +44 sound like nothing else in 2006: two voices trading lines about not wanting to go home, over a bassline with almost nothing to prove.

The history is odd. Hoppus and Barker started +44 as an electronic project with Heller singing lead, and by the time the record was finished she had appeared on one song and gone. What is left of that plan is here. The low end does an electronic job, steady and repetitive, while the melody is carried by people instead of machines.

Play it from the pocket outward. The temptation is to follow the vocal and the part only works if you refuse: same figure, same weight, all the way to the last bar.

It is also the only track on the record with a woman's voice on it, and that single fact has carried the song for twenty years while the rest of the album was discussed for completely different reasons. The duet is not a gimmick bolted onto a breakup record. It is what the band was originally going to sound like, caught once before the plan changed.

Thirteen November 2006, Interscope, three minutes forty four on the record.
```
