## DESCRIPTION (1506 characters)

```text
Track two, and the shortest thing on the record. Class of 2020 came out in the August of that year, eleven tracks, no guest list and no label machinery behind it - Jahred Gomes produced the whole thing himself and the title is the only explanation the album offers for why it sounds like this. Watch It Burn sits directly after the opener and it does the job a second track is supposed to do: it tells you how bad the mood is going to get.

The band had been doing this for over twenty years by then, through a major label deal, a very public walk away from it, and a run of independent records that kept the same audience. That history is why the song works. It is not a young band discovering politics. It is a band that has watched the machinery from the inside for two decades, arriving at the conclusion in under three minutes.

For the bass there is one job: keep the floor solid while the voice does the arguing. Rap metal lives or dies on the pocket, and this one is tighter than most of the genre because nobody was in the room to smooth it out.

Twenty one August 2020, self-produced, two minutes fifty four.
```
