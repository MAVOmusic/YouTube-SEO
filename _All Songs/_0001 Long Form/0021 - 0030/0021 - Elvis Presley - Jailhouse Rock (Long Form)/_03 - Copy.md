## DESCRIPTION (1151 characters)

```text
Recorded in April 1957 in one room at Radio Recorders in Hollywood, released that September, and it sat at number one for seven weeks. Leiber and Stoller wrote it for the film. Two minutes and twenty eight seconds, no intro tape, no second take anyone remembers.

Bill Black is on upright double bass and he is not decorating anything. The line walks, one note per beat, and it stays out of the way of the guitar riff everyone actually hums. Because the upright was recorded in the same room as the drums, the bottom end bleeds into the snare and that is the whole sound - a band standing in a room, not a stack of separate tracks glued together afterwards. Playing it on an electric bass means resisting the urge to add anything the original never had.
```
