## DESCRIPTION (1187 characters)

```text
You have heard this bassline in a dark room before you ever knew there was a band attached to it. That is what Tarantula is: a club twelve first, out on Breakbeat Kaos in June 2005, and only then an album track when Hold Your Colour landed four weeks later.

It was built out of things other people had already recorded. The Tenor Fly verse was recycled from a Rawhill Cru record through DJ Fresh's own stems, and the toasting gives the whole thing a ragga spine over a straight drum and bass roll. $pyda, whose nickname was Black Tarantula, is probably where the title came from.

The bass is why it is here. At this tempo the low end is not decoration, it is the melody, and the vocal rides on a figure that barely changes. There is nothing to hide a drifting hand behind.
```
