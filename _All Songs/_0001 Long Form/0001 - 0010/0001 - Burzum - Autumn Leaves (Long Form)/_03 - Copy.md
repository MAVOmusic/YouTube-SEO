There is a version of this piece that runs four and a half minutes. This one runs sixty-two, and the extra time is the entire point.

There is no riff here to learn and nothing to count. The low end holds a single floor tone while the synth line drifts above it, so the whole job is steadiness. Played on a four string with the tone rolled almost shut, because anything brighter breaks the spell the record is going for.
