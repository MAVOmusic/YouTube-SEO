## DESCRIPTION (1531 characters)

```text
Most of this song is in German. The part everybody remembers is in English, and that is the joke. We're all living in Amerika, Amerika ist wunderbar - a chant simple enough for a stadium full of people to sing along with while the verses underneath it describe exactly what they are singing along to.

It was recorded in 2003 at El Cortijo in Malaga, produced by Jacob Hellner with the band, and released in September 2004 off Reise, Reise, the follow-up to Mutter. The video is the other half of the argument: the band in Apollo-era spacesuits on a soundstage moon, planting a flag, selling the whole mythology back to the country it came from. Twenty years on it reads less like satire and more like a documentary.

Bass-wise this is the least busy thing Oliver Riedel plays on the record and that is deliberate. The verses are almost a straight march, the guitars carry the weight, and the only movement is one small slide up into the chorus, and that is what gives it the swing. Play it heavier and it turns into a football song. Play it exactly as written and it stays a warning.
```
