## DESCRIPTION (1256 characters)

```text
Three minutes and two seconds at 178 beats per minute, and it has been the last song of the night for seventeen years. Nothing else in this catalogue has that job.

It came out as the second single in May 2008, sixth in the running order on So Wrong, It's Right, on Hopeless Records. Alex Gaskarth wrote it with the rest of the band, and it did what no earlier All Time Low song had managed: it charted. Platinum in the US seven years later, which for a pop punk band from Maryland is the whole story in one certificate.

The bass part is why it works live. It is almost entirely eighth notes locked to Rian Dawson's kick, with one walk-up into each chorus that the entire crowd sings back at the band. Nothing clever, nothing to rehearse for a month, and it has outlasted every technical song the scene produced that year.
```
