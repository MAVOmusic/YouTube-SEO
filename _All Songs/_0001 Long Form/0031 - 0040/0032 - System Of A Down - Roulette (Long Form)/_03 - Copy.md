## DESCRIPTION (1435 characters)

```text
Say the name out loud and you hear a wall: down-tuned guitars, drums like a dropped crate, Serj Tankian switching between a scream and a nursery rhyme mid-bar. Roulette has none of that. It is acoustic fingerpicking in plain E, a string pad doing the harmony, and a full song's worth of restraint on a record that was assembled out of leaked sessions.

It sits at track fifteen on Steal This Album!, the 2002 collection built from the band's own unfinished material after the Toxicity sessions escaped. Rick Rubin and Daron Malakian produced it. Malakian wrote the music and shares the vocal with Tankian in the chorus, which is the only reason the second voice sounds like a second person rather than a second character.

The bass on this one barely qualifies as a bass part. Shavo Odadjian is holding the floor, one note at a time, following the chord rather than driving it. That is the whole exercise and it is much harder than it looks: nothing to hide behind, nowhere to be loud, and the mix will expose every dead note.

Three minutes twenty one, one take, no edits. The acoustic is in standard tuning. They almost never play in standard.
```
