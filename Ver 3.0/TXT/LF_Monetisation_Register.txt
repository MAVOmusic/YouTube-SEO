## CORRECTION 01-11-2026 - THE "BOT TRAFFIC" READING WAS WRONG, AND IT WAS MINE
################################################################################
MAVO, after reading the X-Kid finding: "Done back in the day Fiverr campaigns so
that might tell about weird sites and fake engagements. I ordered boost but had
no idea where it coming from. Later on same with YouTube Adsense Ads from Studio.
I paid for youtube to advertise my videos, the source unknown mostly. So on high
view long form videos noise or weird sources can be those."

WHAT I GOT WRONG: I described the X-Kid referrers as an unexplained attack on the
channel ("the traffic is not a real audience", spam and adult-tube domains
arriving from nowhere). The correct reading is that this is **PAID PROMOTION MAVO
BOUGHT DELIBERATELY** - Fiverr boost services at one point, YouTube Ads bought in
Studio later. Nobody attacked anything. The referrer domains are where cheap view
suppliers actually deliver from, and MAVO did not control the supply.

WHY THE OLD READING STILL MATTERED, AND STILL DOES: whether the traffic was
bought or delivered uninvited, it is **not organic demand**, so it still cannot be
counted in organic averages or used to judge whether a format works. The
classification survives. The attribution does not.

WHAT CHANGES IN THE ANALYSIS:
  1. This is a **spend** question, not a security question. MAVO paid for these
     views. The right question is whether they returned anything.
  2. **The paid traffic did not buy what it was bought for.** On X-Kid: 3.0k
     impressions bought or generated, CTR 1.6%, and an average view duration of
     0:16. Marketing agencies sell views; views are what arrived. Watch time,
     retention, ranking and revenue did not come with them.
  3. **Paid low-retention views can actively hurt.** YouTube's recommender reads
     retention as a quality signal. Feeding a video a large block of viewers who
     leave in sixteen seconds teaches the system that the video does not hold
     people, which is the opposite of what promotion is supposed to do. Stated as
     a hypothesis, because we cannot see inside the recommender - but the
     direction is consistent with X-Kid and Black Parade both carrying 36-44%
     recommendation-driven impressions and the worst retention in the set.
  4. **It explains the "Direct or unknown" and "Other YouTube features" lines**
     that appear on the 2024 videos and on nothing in the 2022 set.

NEW RULE FOR REPORTING (replaces the "bot traffic" label):
**PAID-PROMOTION INFLUENCE.** A video is in this class when it shows large
external or browse traffic alongside geography that does not match the channel
(India 60-80%), retention collapse, and low CTR on high impressions. Mark such
videos as **paid-influenced**, exclude their numbers from organic averages, never
quote their view or subscriber totals as evidence that a format works, and say so
plainly rather than implying fraud. Contracted view suppliers are not an attack
and should never be described as one.
