## What the numbers suggest

- 0006 still earns from search nearly four years on, and the top query is the game plus the artist. Keeping Rocksmith, the artist and the song in the title and first description line matters more than a story opener, which is how the proposed title is built.
- Playlists at 17.4% is the strongest of the MIW long forms reviewed, which supports leaving 0006 in the Play Along playlist and out of the genre lists.
- 0147 needs the click, not more impressions: 892 impressions at 0.9%. Its live tags hold seven channel words and nothing about the song, so the tag set is the largest single fix there.
- Both Shorts keep about a third of viewers. 1892 gets more than twice the reach of 0186 with a similar retention, and its geographies lean United States while 0186 leans United Kingdom.
- End screen click rate is 0.0% on all four against a 1.1% channel average. Worth testing, no cause claimed.
