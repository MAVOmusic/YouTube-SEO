LONG-FORM ACCURACY AND ORIGINALITY RELEASE GATE - 2026-11-03

HARD RULE: Before posting ANY video copy, correction, facts or genre list to MAVO,
check each video independently and every surface together: title, description,
tags, pinned comment, genres and any accompanying factual explanation. Do not
claim a whole batch passed because one check passed. Never fill an evidence gap
with a plausible assumption. If a fact cannot be established, omit it or clearly
identify it as unverified outside paste-ready copy. A question/CTA can contain a
false premise or an unsupported offer - verify those too. Identify historical
screenshots as historical, not current live settings. Respect all existing rules,
including actual bass performance with an on-screen overlay. Where image reading
is required and unavailable, stop; do not infer image content.

For each song, research original sources where possible and triangulate; record
claim, exact source URL or repository file/user instruction and assessment, and
distinguish song-level evidence, album context and interpretation. Verify tags,
format, tuning, instrument, dates, track positions, artist spelling, URLs, genre,
questions, CTA promises and analytics. Genre recommendations are reasoned,
source-supported classifications, not provable objective absolutes. Remove claims
that cannot be justified. Revisit the first 70 rather than assuming earlier copy
or partial review is approved. The previous first-ten handoff was a limited review,
not a comprehensive guarantee. When changes affect a surface already delivered,
give the entire replacement block, not fragments.

UNIQUENESS: Every video needs its own researched factual angle, writing and CTA,
even 100 uploads of the same song. Track angles and source against the full
available catalogue and shape registers. Do not invent a new fact to meet this
requirement. If reliable distinct facts or genuinely distinct phrasing cannot be
found, stop and tell MAVO rather than repeat or fabricate. Shared necessary artist,
song, factual terminology and links are not automatically violations. Review
semantic repetition, structure, angle and CTA across the corpus manually; n-gram
signals cannot detect paraphrase or establish whether an overlap is acceptable.
Review previously delivered/live copy if available; the repository may not match
live YouTube. Do not silently claim coverage of material that is inaccessible.

COMMANDS - from repository root, for EVERY video to be released:
  python3 'Ver 3.0/PY/long_form_gate.py' --report 0001 0002
  # Complete research/review; create a record for each video (format below).
  python3 'Ver 3.0/PY/long_form_gate.py' --check 0001 0002
Use actual video IDs in place of example IDs. Report scans repository _03 - Copy.md
long-form copies, title/description/pin across fields, and reports matching five-word
phrases. Tags, genres, other wording similarities, fact-angle duplication and CTA
shapes need manual comparison. Output is a warning list to examine, not proof of
originality. The check fails for missing or stale hashes/records, malformed review,
or missing review attestations; the reviewer must actually do the work. If a file
or comparison corpus changes, rerun report and review affected collision signals.
A changed comparison corpus with the same report can still change semantic
originality: manually recheck it even if the hash happens to remain the same.

Create UTF-8 JSON at Ver 3.0/TXT/Long_Form_Reviews/NNNN.json, with:
{
  "reviewer": "person/agent who performed the review",
  "reviewed_at": "date and time",
  "surface_sha256": {"title": "hash from report", "description": "hash from report", "tags": "hash from report", "pinned comment": "hash from report", "genres": "hash from report"},
  "reviewed_surfaces": {"title": true, "description": true, "tags": true, "pinned comment": true, "genres": true},
  "claims": [{"surface": "description", "claim": "specific assertion", "source": "exact URL or source path and relevant location", "assessment": "what source establishes, including limitations"}],
  "distinct_fact_and_source": "distinct angle, source, and why different from other uses of song",
  "uniqueness_assessment": "reviewed collisions and semantic/shape/CTA comparison, noting unavoidable overlaps",
  "uncertainties_and_removed_claims": "what cannot be established and what was removed, or explain none",
  "collision_report_sha256": "hash from report",
  "all_claims_checked": true,
  "cross_song_uniqueness_checked": true
}
Claims is an item for EACH factual claim, implication and promise across all
surfaces (including genre and tag claims), not one generic source per song. Record
external source retrieval and limitations. Do not create records merely to make
the code pass. The example is a schema, not a valid review or evidence.

This gate cannot verify truth, completeness of a claim inventory, source quality,
semantic uniqueness or the user's current YouTube copy automatically. A green
result only says the specified records and hashes exist and agree; an agent must
independently assess facts and review all material. The existing verify.sh covers
bands/ packages only and must never be cited as approval of long-form files.

COMMENTS: every video needs a creator comment carrying a fact, quiz, trivia or sourced question, whether or not a viewer or artist comment is already pinned. A preserved pin does not replace it: supply both surfaces, and post the creator comment as its own comment. See TXT/Creator_Comment_Rule.md.

Preserved artist comments: where MAVO elects to keep a real artist's comment,
write an explicit PINNED COMMENT heading saying preserve / do not replace and a
single blockquote line beginning `> PRESERVED ARTIST COMMENT: ` followed by the
observed author and comment. The gate hashes that line as the pin surface;
it is not a paste block and must not be posted as the creator's own comment.
This marker does not establish present pin status or prove who authored it.
