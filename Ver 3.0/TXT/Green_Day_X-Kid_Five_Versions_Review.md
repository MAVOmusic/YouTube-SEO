## What the numbers say

- **0027 is a paid-carrying video.** 93.8% of its traffic is YouTube advertising and only 0.3% is YouTube search. The 0.5% click-through on 884 impressions is the real problem, and no description rewrite fixes that. The copy still puts the song, artist, album and chart words where search and the first snippet line can see them.
- **0008 is carried off YouTube.** 79.1% external, with xmobox.pro, tboxx.com and desitales2.com as the top referrers, and only 3.0% search. Its audience is 77.6% India and 90.2% male, and 0:16 of 4:05 average view duration is the weakest retention on the channel's X-Kid set. The copy explains the play-along format immediately rather than burying it, because a viewer arriving cold needs to know what they are watching inside the first line.
- **0010 is the strongest search Short of the three.** 12.8% search against 4.6% and 6.7%, and the top two search terms are the song and artist in both orders. The old pipe title and the hashtag paragraph are both replaced.
- **1524 and 1772 are feed-led twins.** 90.8% and 90.0% Shorts feed, both 0:36, both published two and a half months apart, and both currently carrying the *identical* title `Green Day - X-Kid #rocksmith #bass #greenday` with the same 2022 description and the same ten legacy tags. 1772 outperforms at 929 views and 0:21 average view duration. The duplicate title is the clearest thing to fix on this channel.
- All five sit at 0.0% end screen click rate against a 1.1% average. An end screen is worth trying, but no past cause is claimed.
