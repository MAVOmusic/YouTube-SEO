## The duplicate columns, and why they matter

The `All long form ID` and `All Shorts ID` columns carry every catalogue row for that song, not just the pair being uploaded. The planned pair sits inside them, and the rest are the earlier uploads of the same song, which is what the duplicates are.

- **First version**: the song has no other row in either catalogue, so this is its first appearance on the channel.
- **Several IDs**: the song exists already, sometimes three or four times. Example: Motionless In White - Another Life has long forms 0002, 0004 and 0033 and is now paired again at 0784/2531.
- Older versions keep their own IDs and their own copy. Nothing is merged, renamed or reused, and the new pair never takes an old number.
