## How to record each test

Log previous Short and pair, public release date/time and timezone, screenshot timestamp, lifetime views, feed traffic/source and time first observed, first observation of >=500, next actual pair release and readiness, restrictions and relevant promotion. Also keep 24h/48h/7d metrics at matched ages where available, plus engaged views, retention, geography and LF performance; a total view threshold alone says nothing about retention, subscriber conversion or whether the next upload affected the previous Short. Since 31 March 2025 Shorts views include starts/replays; engaged views are distinct: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en . Missing source data is unknown, not zero. This conditional sequencing is an experiment, not proof that crossing 500 causes more feed distribution. Song, format, day, audience, promotion and timing confound comparisons.
