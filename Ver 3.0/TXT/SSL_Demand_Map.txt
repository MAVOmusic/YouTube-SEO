2. THE HONEST ANSWER ON "MAXIMUM GO VIRAL CHANCE"
################################################################################
I cannot rank this library by viral probability, and neither can anything else.
What I can do is tell you what the channel's own numbers say, because they say
something unusual.

*** CORRECTION 01-11-2026, MAVO: THE 382.3k IS NOT ORGANIC ***
I wrote in v1 of this map that the SOAD figure was "ORGANIC, unexplained". MAVO
corrected it: "That SOAD song is long form and my biggest ad campaign."
So it is his biggest campaign, not a breakout, and this map has been rewritten
around that. It was the one number in this project that looked like proof that
content could travel by itself, and it is not.

THE THREE BIGGEST NUMBERS IN THE CORPUS - ALL THREE ARE NOW NON-ORGANIC:
    System Of A Down - Lonely Day ... 382,300 ... biggest AD CAMPAIGN (MAVO)
    Taking Back Sunday - Cute W... ..  10,723 ... 86.6% paid advertising
    Slayer - War Ensemble ...........   1,694 ... 98.4% paid advertising
    Frederic Chopin - 4-hour loop ...   4,887 ... 60.9% news-suggested
    Play along series (best single) ... ~1,500 ... genuine search, 60-70% of views

Read that list again, because it is the most important thing in this document.
Every large number this channel has ever produced was bought, borrowed from a news
cycle, or is a Short. The genuine organic ceiling on a long form is around
1,000-1,500 views, and that is what thirty-four months of free search traffic
actually delivers.

WHAT THAT MEANS FOR SONG SELECTION, STATED PLAINLY:
    - There is no song in this 49,891-song library with a demonstrated organic
      breakout behind it. Not one. So "what will go viral" cannot be answered from
      this data, by me or by anyone.
    - Buying views works: it reliably moves the counter. What it does not do is
      convert. CO-0012 converted 0.79% of views to subs, CO-0013 0.71%, against 8.16%
      for the free search traffic of the play along series. A bought view is worth
      roughly a tenth of an organic one.
    - The only lever with evidence behind it is the one this channel already owns:
      a song people search for, in a format that starts on the music. That is a small
      claim, but it is a real one, and it is repeatable.

THE ONE THING THE SOAD NUMBER STILL TELLS US, AND IT IS A DIFFERENT THING NOW:
it tells us where MAVO chose to spend his largest budget. He put it behind a nu-metal
bassline rather than a ballad or a metal classic, and he has 62 SOAD songs in the
library with 3 packaged. That is a bet he made, not a result he got. Worth knowing,
not worth building a strategy on.

4. TIER C - PLAY FOR TRAFFIC, EXPECT E4
################################################################################
Major-published modern catalogue. Content ID will match. 23 of 23 audited long forms
are ineligible and every single one of these will join them.

This is not a reason to avoid them. It is the reason to stop expecting money from
them. Their value is search traffic (60-70% of views on the play along series) and
subscriber conversion (11-29%).
