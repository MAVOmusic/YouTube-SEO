2. THE HONEST ANSWER ON "MAXIMUM GO VIRAL CHANCE"
################################################################################
I cannot rank this library by viral probability, and neither can anything else.
What I can do is tell you what the channel's own numbers say, because they say
something unusual.

THE THREE BIGGEST NUMBERS IN THE CORPUS, AND WHAT EACH ONE ACTUALLY IS:

    System Of A Down - Lonely Day (Short) ... 382,300 views ... ORGANIC, unexplained
    Taking Back Sunday - Cute Without E ....  10,723 views ... 86.6% PAID
    Frederic Chopin - Nocturne 4-hour loop .   4,887 views ... 60.9% news-suggested

Only one of those three came from people finding the video on purpose. Only one of
them came from the content itself doing something. And it is a SHORT, not a long
form.

WHAT THAT MEANS FOR SONG SELECTION:
    - Buying views produces views and nothing else. It does not produce search
      traffic, subscribers who stay, or a single view after the money stops.
    - A loop can pick up thousands of views from a news cycle it has no relationship
      with, and still earn 54 pence.
    - The thing that has genuinely broken out once, on its own, is a 60-second
      snippet of a very famous nu-metal bassline.

If you want my actual read: **the variable is not the song, it is the format and the
first three seconds.** SOAD - Lonely Day is a Short. The five play alongs that opened
on the music held 62-70% at 0:30. The three that opened on a score card held 10-19%.
Cute Without the E held 19% at 0:30 with a hard cut. The song list decides what is
worth playing. The opening decides whether anyone meets it.

THE ONE SONG-LEVEL SIGNAL THAT IS REAL, AND IT IS HUGE:
On CO-0012's "Viewers also watch" panel - which is YouTube telling you what your own
audience does - the top entry is:

    System Of A Down - Lonely Day (Bass) #rocksmith - 382.3k views, 1 year ago

SYSTEM OF A DOWN HAS 62 SONGS IN THE LIBRARY AND 3 PACKAGED. 59 are unused, including
Chop Suey!, B.Y.O.B., Aerials, Toxicity-era material and the rest.
This is the single most defensible recommendation in this document: the channel has
proven, once, that a SOAD bass part can do 382,000 views. Nothing else in 12 audits
comes within 35x of that.
