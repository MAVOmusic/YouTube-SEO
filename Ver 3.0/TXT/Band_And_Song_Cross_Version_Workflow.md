# Band and song cross-version workflow - 8 November 2026

For every video analysis, search the entire available Long Form and Short Form catalogues and the artist's band folder. Check artist aliases, accented and plain names, song spelling and variant editions; record exact indexed IDs and separately flag plausible or uncertain matches. Index entries, title strings and reservations do not prove a video is public, paired, the same audio recording, or even correctly identified. Catalogue gaps mean absence is not proof.

Maintain a detailed artist-level map and song-level notes in `Band Info/<artist>/`: all indexed songs and versions, source paths, snapshot dates, relevant metrics and denominators, original/current status uncertainty, titles and copy locations, genres with sources, researched facts, meaningful tags and misspellings with evidence, real comments/pins, link targets, content/rights uncertainties, decisions and next checks. Link instead of duplicating large evidence where practical. Distinguish user-reported actions, screenshot observations, research and proposals. Record full mapping as the known index, including limitations; keep it updated whenever a new item is found or reviewed.

Before deciding title, description, genres, tags or pinned comment, compare the same song and all relevant prior artist copy and evidence. Any new finding may inform older versions as well as the new one: document whether a proposed old change is warranted, no change is warranted, or decision is pending more evidence. Do not bulk-edit earlier videos without checking their copy, performance, comments and live state. Retain distinct angles and preserve genuine artist/viewer engagement. Shorts are normally reviewed individually unless explicitly overridden. Follow LF release gate and screenshot vision requirements when examining actual screenshots. Never invent a source, Studio edit or link target.

The eight `Video_Map.md` files made for LF 0101-0110 are full scans of the currently accessible 1000/3000 catalogues for those artists, not full-channel certification; refresh them on later catalogue changes. LF 0101 is reviewed; 0102-0110 and matching Shorts are pending.
