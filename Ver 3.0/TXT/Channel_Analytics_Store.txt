--- G. READ (what it means) -------------------------------------------------

G1  THE CHANNEL IS A SHORTS-ONLY, SEARCH-FED, NEW-VIEWER TREADMILL.
    95.1% of views are Shorts; 86% of viewers never watch a long-form; 88.6% are new;
    97.1% of watch time is non-subscribers; a single Short = 63.6% of all remaining
    watch time on the channel (009 Dreamscape in the 90d "growing your audience" old
    data aside). Verdict: the long-form library contributes almost nothing today.

G2  SEARCH IS THE WHOLE ENGINE - 82.6% of views.
    This is the single most important number in the capture. Nobody arrives from the
    Shorts feed (3.9%) or Suggested (not even listed at channel level). Search means
    KEYWORD MATCHING, and it is why the top-10 is populated by titles that literally
    contain "Artist - Song #rocksmith #bass".

G3  THE NEWEST UPLOAD PROVES THE KEYWORD LOSS.
    "That dark, fuzz-drenched Do I Wanna Know? riff is peak 2010s indie!" contains the
    song but NOT the artist ("Arctic Monkeys"), and it carries no platform token.
    90 views in 16 h against a 790-1,100 typical band = ~90% under-performance, at
    CTR 1.2%. A bespoke-only title cannot be matched to an "arctic monkeys bass" or
    "rocksmith bass" query. This is the title contract fix in CONTEXT §32.

G4  WE ARE OPTIMISING THE WRONG SONGS.
    8 of the top 10 traffic earners have NO package (009 Dreamscape, Chicago, 9mm
    Inferno, Sabaton - En Livstid I Krig, Primus, Alestorm - Rum, Maximum The Hormone).
    The workspace's 308 packages were built in ID order, not traffic order. Only 110
    have ever been audited. Work order must invert: package/repair what YouTube is
    ALREADY serving (see §32 "traffic-first queue").

G5  VIEW->FOLLOWER CONVERSION IS NEGATIVE.
    Gross +6 subs in 28 days against a NET -197. YouTube is removing/cleaning subs
    (109,954 live vs the 110,700+ in the Ver 2.68 record) while content adds almost
    none. The bell is effectively dead: 428 total-notify (0.4%) vs a 10-30% norm.
    Anything that depends on subscribers (premieres, notifications, LF launches) has
    no distribution base right now. Growth must be 100% discovery-driven.

G6  THE HOOK IS THE PRODUCT - AVD IS ALL OVER THE PLACE.
    0:18-0:22 (52-62%) is the normal band; Sabaton 173.7% shows genuine looping
    (a repeatable hit shape); Alestorm 0:06 / 17.9% and the 0:06 Alestorm-class are
    hook failures - the short gives away nothing before the swipe. Record AVD% per
    Short and treat <35% as a rewrite-the-opening trigger.

G7  THUMBNAIL/CTR IS THE BIGGEST MECHANICAL LEVER FOR LONG-FORM.
    190.6k impressions at 1.2% CTR = 2.3k engaged views. A move to 3-4% would triple
    long-form reach without a single new upload. (Shorts CTR is less actionable, but
    the same art discipline applies.)

G8  REVENUE IS COLLAPSING AND SHORTS IS THE CAUSE.
    Aug 3.30 -> Oct 0.86 (-74%) on ~10k views/28d, 96% of it Shorts Feed ads, video
    RPM 0.08. There is no money in Shorts at this volume. Long-form (US 25.5% traffic,
    25-44 male) is the only path back to meaningful RPM - which reinforces the new
    upload plan, not Shorts volume.

G9  THE FIX LIST IS NOW UNAMBIGUOUS.
    (1) resume cadence - YouTube names it as the cause of the view drop;
    (2) restore artist+song keyword strings into every title (§32 contract);
    (3) traffic-first queue: package the 8 top earners + the live tail first;
    (4) thumbnail/CTR programme on long-form;
    (5) per-Short AVD% ledger with a <35% rewrite trigger;
    (6) build long-form as the revenue lane, Shorts as the discovery lane.
    Target math: 109,954 subs, 869 views/48h today; the standing goal is 1,000+ views
    per HOUR -> x55. Currently 35 views/hour.
