## Genre list for Green Day

The canonical row for 0008 is Punk Rock, Pop Punk, Alternative Rock, Skate Punk and Power Pop, used artist-wide across the catalogue rather than as a track-by-track classification.

None of those five genres appears in `Ver 3.0/TXT/Playlist_Inventory.json`. The registry holds 23 playlists: All Genres, Ambient, Dungeon Synth, Black Metal, Atmospheric Black Metal, Dark Ambient, Metalcore, Gothic Metal, Industrial Metal, Nu Metal, Hard Rock and the Play Along long-form list. So on the registry alone, ten Green Day genre playlists are missing, five genres in two formats.

Confirm in Studio before creating any of them, in case a list exists under a different name. Nothing here is evidence that a playlist is absent from the channel, only that it is absent from the registry.

## Separate gap worth knowing

The registry also has no Shorts counterpart for the play-along series. Dedicated play-alongs go to the Play Along playlist and stay out of the genre lists, and Shorts 0010, 1524 and 1772 are play-along Shorts, so they have nowhere to go right now. If that list should exist, the matching title would be `Rocksmith 2014 Bass Play Along Shorts` with the same three-link description shape. Not created, and not part of the ten above.

## Notes

- Five genres in two formats is ten playlists. If that is more than you want to maintain, Pop Punk and Punk Rock carry Green Day most directly, Power Pop and Skate Punk are the narrower fits for this band, and Alternative Rock overlaps with the MCR lists that already exist under a different genre set.
- Genre membership is a placement decision, not a claim that every Green Day track sits in all five genres. The canonical list is artist-wide.
