## Why these windows

The six windows are the ones already used on this channel: 02:00 and 05:00 catch the previous US evening, 13:30 and 16:00 sit in the European afternoon and the India evening, 19:00 and 23:00 reach the European evening and the US afternoon. YouTube states that publish time is not known to affect long-term performance, so these are consistency windows that make like-for-like comparisons possible, not a claim that any hour is better: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/141805?hl=en
