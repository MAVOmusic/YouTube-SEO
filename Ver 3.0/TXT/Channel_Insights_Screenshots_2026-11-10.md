## What this changes, and what it does not

- The time slots are chosen against the heatmap bands and the geography ranking. Full reasoning in `Ver 3.0/TXT/Upload_Time_Reasoning.md`.
- The 84 to 6 split between Shorts and Videos is the reason both formats still ship together: the Short is the reach vehicle and the long form is the destination the Short points at.
- Search at 38.6% is the reason titles lead with the song and artist.
- None of these numbers is a promise. They describe 28 days in which a run of pop-punk and alt-rock Shorts did the work.
