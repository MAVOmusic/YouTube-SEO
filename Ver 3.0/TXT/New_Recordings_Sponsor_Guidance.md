# New recording sponsor disclosure - research note

User reports payment to display two sponsor banners on stream, Vodra continuously and another periodically, and asks about YouTube uploads including clips. A paid brand overlay can be branded content even if never spoken about. For each upload or Short which actually retains such paid brand promotion, conservatively declare paid promotion in YouTube Studio; review clips individually. If a banner is absent and the deal doesn't otherwise cover or influence that upload, its status needs evaluation rather than blanket assumptions. Review sponsor contracts and whether both campaigns cover reposts. Never imply the sponsor paid specifically for YouTube exposure without evidence.

YouTube's branded content policy requires declaration for content influenced by a brand partner in exchange for value: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/17596007?hl=en . Its help page describes Studio Content > video > Show more > paid promotion checkbox and says it adds a disclosure at the start; ads may still run: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/154235?hl=en-GB . New-upload wording may vary. Mark affected live streams, long videos and Shorts individually, including already published affected items if applicable. No API/Studio change has been made.

Declaring does not independently approve ad format. YouTube prohibits embedded advertiser-created video ads or other commercial breaks and third-party ads comparable to YouTube ad formats: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3364658?hl=en-GB . The provided description doesn't establish whether overlays are static, animated, creator-made or supplied ads, or whether the periodic placement resembles a separate break. Do not claim a continuously displayed or recurring banner is explicitly permitted. Review actual footage, contract and YouTube rules and seek clarification from YouTube if uncertain; removing problematic ad assets and adjusting the sponsor arrangement may be necessary.

UK advertising guidance requires advertising to be obviously identifiable, and for largely editorial videos with advertising a clear disclosure at or immediately before the advertising, for example prominent on-screen text: https://www.asa.org.uk/news/like-comment-and-comply-youtube-and-the-cap-code.html . Use an upfront clear Ad label and clearly identify intermittent paid promotion where it appears; the Studio checkbox alone is not a guarantee of UK legal compliance. Depending on contract, audience and jurisdiction further duties may apply; seek ASA/CAP advice or legal advice when uncertain.
