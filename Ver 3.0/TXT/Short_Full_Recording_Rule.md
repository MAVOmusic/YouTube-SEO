# Short rule: point at the full recording, and do not describe it as a cut

Two standing instructions from the user, first written down 2026-11-10 after they had to repeat them.

## 1. The Short description and the Short pin must point at the full recording

Every Short carries the attached full recording, which is the matching long form connected as the related video. The copy has to say so in two places:

1. **In the description**, as its own block.
2. **At the top of the pinned creator comment**, on the line above the quiz, fact or trivia.

The order in the comment is fixed. The pointer comes first, the quiz or fact comes after it. Never bury the pointer at the bottom or leave it out because the description already says it.

The pointer names the thing, not a duration: "the attached full recording" or "the full recording is attached below as the related video". The Short's own length is given separately, and the two are never confused, because the Short is 30 to 60 seconds and the full recording is minutes.

## 2. Never describe the Short as trimmed, cut down, lifted or pulled from the long form

That is factually wrong for this channel. The user records one performance with a foot pedal through OBS with the Aitum vertical plugin, capturing a horizontal feed and a vertical feed at the same moment. The two uploads are the same performance from the same take, one landscape and one portrait. Nothing is cut, and nothing is a second take either.

So the copy says neither of these things:

- Wrong: "cut from the full video", "trimmed from the long form", "a section lifted out of the long form".
- Also wrong: "a separate recording", "a different take", "recorded separately".

What it can say: the full recording is attached as the related video. If a line about the two formats is needed at all, it says the same performance was captured in both orientations at the same time. The user's own view is that viewers do not care, so this is a fact to have on file rather than a selling point to lead with.

## 3. Publishing order

The long form goes up as a premiere at the slot time and the Short publishes five minutes later, for example 23:00 premiere and 23:05 Short. The reason is procedural:

- A long form cannot take a comment until it is published or set to premiere.
- A long form cannot be connected to a Short until it is published or set to premiere.

So the premiere is set first, the long form takes its pinned creator comment, the Short is then connected to it as the related video, and the Short publishes five minutes after the premiere starts. In Studio the premiere counts as available, so the connection can be made before it actually starts.

## 4. The pointer wording is never reused

Correction 2026-11-11: the pointer is required on every Short, but the sentence that carries it must be written fresh for that video, in the description and again, differently, at the top of the pin. Reusing one pointer sentence across a batch is the same repetition fault as a reused title or CTA line. Naming the song in the pointer is the easiest way to keep it unique.

The pointer also does not restate the song title. The description already names the song once, so the pointer and the pin opening say "the whole song", "the full length performance" or "the complete performance" instead. Correction 2026-11-11, second pass: all three Pins And Needles packages (LF 0688, Short 2435, Short 0725) opened their titles with the same "Pins And Needles on bass:" skeleton and repeated the title inside the pointer lines.
