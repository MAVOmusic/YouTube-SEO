## The seven angles, so nothing repeats

1. **0009**: the eleven-note piano intro starting on G5, and Rob Cavallo's scrapped album opener about a marching band crossing a city that was folded into this song.
2. **0167**: the UK number one, the band's only one, against the United States, where it topped Modern Rock but the all-genre Hot 100 told a different story.
3. **0673**: the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame listing among songs that shaped rock and roll, the 2025 Rolling Stone placing at 96, and the US and UK platinum certifications.
4. **0068**: Time ranking it third among the best songs of 2006, with the Rolling Stone 17th and BBC 43rd placings held back as quiz answers.
5. **1434**: the G major to A major shift and the 75 to 194 half-time tempo.
6. **1912**: El Dorado Recording Studios in Burbank, with Capitol Studios as the near miss.
7. **2420**: label staff wanting Famous Last Words or Teenagers as the lead single instead.

Three facts were deliberately dropped because the same uploads already carried them: the MTV Video Music Awards on 0167, the UK chart placing on 0673, and the Samuel Bayer video on 1912, where the only live comment is the creator's own line about the director. The MCR Rocksmith song pack, already on 1434's creator comment, is not reused either.

## Limits

- The 50-intervening-upload comparison remains paused at the user's request. The full accessible corpus was checked instead, which is not the same guarantee.
- The song title is itself a five-word phrase, so every package contains it. The check excludes it deliberately and reports the residual signals rather than suppressing them.
- Screenshots show displayed values, and Save and Undo state do not prove saved metadata. Comments are historical, and the folders were refreshed on 2026-11-10, so displayed counts may already have moved on.
- 0009 runs 3:33 against a 5:11 album cut and a 4:37 radio edit, so the copy says the arrangement is shorter than the record and never calls it the radio edit.
- 0673's search term `dirantai digelangi rindu` and 0009's top referrer nwrtbbt.com are unrelated traffic noise. Neither is used to justify a tag or a line of copy.
- Pinning a comment, editing a title, adding tags or adding a playlist membership are Studio actions for the user, not evidence that anything happened.
- No viewer or creator comment text is changed anywhere, and no Studio action was taken.
