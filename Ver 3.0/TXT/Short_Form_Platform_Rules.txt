SHORT-FORM PLATFORM AND EDITORIAL RULES - 2026-11-04

Sources are YouTube Help; confirm policy again when implementing later.

1. Ordinary URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments, including pinned comments, are non-clickable. Avoid pasting them as a click CTA. Channel profile links are clickable; only direct viewers to named links there after checking they are configured. Do not falsely imply the short description links are clickable. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639?hl=en
2. A Short can have one related item selected from the creator's own channel, including videos, Shorts or Live content. It appears as a clickable link below the handle. Related video requires advanced features; the selected item must be public or unlisted and comply with Community Guidelines. Choose the user's well-performing matching full version when it exists; otherwise choose a relevant worthwhile catalogue video, checking availability and evidence of performance. Never imply a different song is a full version of the Short. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en
3. User confirms Short 0001 related video points to its full version. Every future Short gets a distinctive, sourced trivia fact and a distinct related-video CTA in its pin. Verify current artist comments and prefer an authentic artist comment when present. Uniqueness is an editorial requirement, not a guarantee against spam classification. YouTube prohibits high-volume repetitive or deceptive comments, including similar channel promotion across hundreds of videos. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en
4. Relevant hashtags may be in the Short's title or description; they are optional, not a prerequisite for Shorts classification or guaranteed reach. No official optimal count is stated. More than 60 hashtags causes all hashtags on that content to be ignored; misleading hashtags and over-tagging may have consequences. Avoid keyword stuffing and irrelevant hashtags. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en
5. Tags in the dedicated tag field are separate from hashtags. YouTube says title, thumbnail and description matter more, and tags generally have minimal discovery impact except for misspellings. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en-GB
6. Shorts longer than one minute with an active Content ID claim are blocked regardless of policy; do not infer a claim, block or strike for a particular video without evidence. A claim is different from a copyright strike. Short 0001 is 32 seconds; that over-one-minute rule alone does not establish its rights status. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6013276?hl=en
7. Keep facts, genre, searchable strings and CTA truthful; avoid repeating signature titles, trivia and comment structures. Log each Short separately; no title without a shape-register row. No code or corpus check proves factual accuracy, originality or future performance.
