## THE REPERTOIRE RULE (NEW VER 3.7 - the real determinant of earnings)
################################################################################
Claim status follows the COMPOSITION, so repertoire choice decides monetisation
before a single frame is recorded or a single word of metadata is written.

  PUBLIC DOMAIN / UNCLAIMED WORKS PAY. Classical (Chopin, and by extension the
  whole pre-1900 canon), traditional, folk, hymn, and any composition whose
  publishing is genuinely unadministered. MR-0004 is the proof.

  CLAIMED CATALOGUES DO NOT PAY. Modern metal/hardcore/alt published by Fearless,
  Roadrunner, Concord, Sony, UMG etc. Every capture of these is E4, whatever the
  metadata says. That is now 15 of 15.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE ROADMAP: long-form income does not come from re-recording
Copyrighted covers better. It comes from pointing the long-form lane at
