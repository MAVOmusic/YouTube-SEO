--- DIAGNOSIS ----------------------------------------------------------------

D1  THE RETENTION NUMBERS ARE NOT A CONTENT SIGNAL.
    38:41 AVD / 62.0% APV on a 1:02:24 ambient loop are inflated by the
    start-soon playback, and the curve proves it: a machine-flat ~33% band for
    the first 31:12 is a background-playback shape, not human viewing. The hard
    fall at 31:12 is the point where the loop stops being someone's waiting room.
    => Do NOT apply AVD/APV badges, "gap ladder" entries or flop/breakout
       verdicts to this video. It is a utility asset. Same class as a playlist
       bed, not a music upload.

D2  YOUTUBE HAS NO IDEA WHAT THIS VIDEO IS. (the actual SEO problem)
    86.4% of views arrive as Suggested - and the top 4 sources are a Venom
    trailer, a Friends reunion trailer, an Indian news stream and a Dom trailer.
    Nothing about the recommendation cluster is music, black metal, ambient,
    dungeon synth or bass. That means the metadata gave the classifier no usable
    topic: the title is a container label, the description is a link wall about
    Kick/Discord/Rumble/TikTok, and the tags are channel-brand + platform +
    "varg" terms. The one real topical keyword (`rocksmith bass`) is only a tag,
    and it is still the #1 search term (13.6%) - so even that weak signal is
    doing work.

D3  THE AUDIENCE BEING DELIVERED IS THE LOWEST-VALUE LANE.
    India 29.4% + Pakistan 2.4% + mobile 90.9% + 95.1% non-subscribed + zero
    revenue. Combined with D2 this is the classic "off-topic recommendation"
    signature from the project doctrine (§ analytics-notes), not a content
    problem - the video is simply being matched to whatever is cheap to serve.

D4  SEARCH IS 0.8% - and the demand is provably there.
    Within that tiny slice `burzum autumn leaves` (9.1%) and `one way autumn
    1 hour` (4.6%) are the second and fifth terms. Evergreen "1 hour loop" demand
    exists and this video - the only 1-hour asset on the channel - covers none of
    it in the title, description or tags. This is the cheapest traffic on the
    channel and it is being left on the table.

D5  "INELIGIBLE TO EARN" - not an SEO issue, and not fixable by metadata.
    The red notice is a claims/policy state on the audio. Left as-is per doctrine
    (no delete/re-upload). Consequence: this video will never produce RPM, so
    optimise it for watch time / playlist utility / thematic accuracy, not money.

D6  DEAD END-SCREEN. 0.0% end-screen CTR vs 1.1% channel average, on a video with
    2,895 views. There is no exit route out of the loop into the channel.

D7  GENRE LABEL IN THE CORPUS IS WRONG FOR THIS SONG.
    The three bands/ packages (0120, 0372, 1791) say "Wikipedia Genres: Black
    metal, atmospheric black metal". 'Autumn Leaves' is track 11 on *The Ways of
    Yore* (2014) - genre Ambient / Dungeon Synth (verified 01-11-2026:
    RateYourMusic lists The Ways of Yore as Ambient, Dungeon Synth; the track is
    acoustic picking over tremolo electrics, no drums). Flagged for the genre gate;
    not corrected yet because it rewrites three shipped packages' description
    genre lines - awaiting MAVO go-ahead.

D8  THE PIN IS A SECOND COPY OF THE DESCRIPTION AND HAS 0 REPLIES.
    It repeats the whole link wall (Kick, Discord, X, TikTok, Rumble, Twitch,
    Super Thanks) - a direct §21 violation (pins must carry ZERO URLs) and a
